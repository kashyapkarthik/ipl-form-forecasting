Position matching helps overall.
Seven separate histories do not help everywhere.

Position-specific shrinkage improves delivery log loss by 0.35% against original all-batter shrinkage on the same 12,881 late-half deliveries. The gain over each position’s population forecast is small and uncertain.

Exploratory follow-up. Positions 1/2 mean initial striker/non-striker, not independently verified scorecard slots. Positions 4–6 and equal-player scoring do not favour the new model. Later experiments remain unchanged.
