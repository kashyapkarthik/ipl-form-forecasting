Opener-specific shrinkage reduces log loss by 0.44% versus original all-batter shrinkage on the same 4,975 late-opening deliveries in 2025–2026. Its advantage over an opener-only population forecast remains uncertain.

This is a follow-up motivated by known original results. It is not a fresh confirmatory test. The 2024-selected wider prior worsens evaluation forecasts. Later experiments and 2027 watchlists are unchanged.
